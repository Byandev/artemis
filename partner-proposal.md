# Artemis — Partner & Equity Proposal

**Prepared by:** Bryan Mulingbayan
**Date:** April 2026

---

## The Story

I built Artemis from an idea into a working product — the first analytics and automation platform purpose-built for Philippine COD e-commerce. Every line of architecture, every integration, every late night was mine. But I didn't do it completely alone. Two people helped along the way, and one company believed in the product early enough to test it.

This document outlines how I propose to recognize those contributions fairly.

- **Network** — introductions to COD sellers and industry contacts

- **Free updates** — all future Artemis features and improvements are included for the beta company's instance, free of charge — no monthly or annual fees

## Operating Costs

Artemis is self-funded by Bryan. Here are the ongoing costs to run the business:

### Fixed Monthly Costs

| Cost | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Server / Infrastructure** | ₱7,500/mo (current) | DigitalOcean droplet + managed MySQL. Scales with client base. |
| **Domain** | ₱5,700/yr (~₱475/mo) | Annual renewal |
| **Full-time Developer** | ₱17,000/mo | Development and client support |
| **Tooling** | ₱3,500/mo | Laravel Forge, S3/Spaces, email, Sentry, PostHog |
| **Total (current)** | **~₱28,500/mo** | Before significant scaling |

### How costs scale

- **Server costs grow with customers.** Currently ~₱7.5k/mo (4GB DB + 8GB app). Expect ~₱15k at 80 customers, ~₱30k at 250, ~₱60k at 500. More clients = more data, more API calls, more processing.
- **Developer hire is in plan.** ~₱17k/mo to free Bryan for product strategy and growth.
- **All operating costs are funded by Bryan** until the business generates enough revenue to cover them. Partners are not expected to contribute financially.

### Break-even context

| Milestone | Customers needed | MRR | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cover operating costs | ~5 | ~₱30k | Server + domain + developer + tooling |
| Profitable operation | ~10 | ~₱60k | All costs covered, healthy margin |

The break-even is dramatically easier than it looks because parcel journey + SMS are bundled into the subscription. Average revenue per customer ₱5,949/mo means even 5 customers covers everything.

## Conditions for All Equity Holders

1. **Bryan retains full ownership of the project.** The Artemis codebase, brand, and IP belong to Bryan. Equity represents a share of the business value, not ownership of the product itself.
2. **Non-compete:** Cannot build or invest in a competing PH COD analytics product while holding equity and 12 months after selling/transferring shares.
3. **IP assignment:** Any work done on Artemis belongs to Bryan / the company, not the individual contributor.
4. **Drag-along / tag-along:** If Bryan sells his majority stake, others must sell too (drag-along). If Bryan receives an offer, others can join on the same terms (tag-along).
5. **Right of first refusal:** If any equity holder wants to sell their shares, Bryan has first right to buy at fair market value.
6. **Beta company instance:** The self-hosted copy with free updates is part of the partnership arrangement and tied to their equity stake.
