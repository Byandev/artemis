# Artemis · Marketing Strategy Plan

> **Mission:** Make Artemis the default RTS-reduction platform for every Philippine COD seller running on Pancake POS.

This plan translates the Artemis positioning ("Hunt down RTS. Protect your margin.") into a concrete go-to-market strategy — audience, messaging, channels, content, funnel, and measurement.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**Product:** Artemis — analytics & automation platform for PH COD e-commerce.
**Core promise:** See your real RTS rate in 2 minutes. Cut it by ~10% (relative) in 90 days.
**Hook:** RTS is the silent margin killer; most sellers can't see their real number.
**Anchor offer:** 14-day free trial · No credit card · Connect 1 Pancake page.
**Lead magnet:** `/rts-calculator` — quantifies the seller's monthly bleed in ₱.

**North Star Metric:** Paid workspaces actively running Parcel Journey notifications (this is where the RTS drop actually happens — not just signups).

---

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary Segment: The "Bleeding Operator"
- **Who:** PH COD seller on Pancake POS, 500–5,000 orders/month
- **Pain:** Knows RTS is hurting them, doesn't know the exact number, no tool to fix it
- **Budget:** ₱5k–₱30k/month for ops tools
- **Where they are:** Facebook groups (Pancake Seller PH, COD Seller PH), TikTok ads, IG
- **Decision trigger:** Saw a month where RTS spiked; lost a supplier; can't scale profitably

### Secondary Segment: The "Scaling Brand"
- **Who:** Multi-SKU seller, 5k–50k orders/month, small ops team (2–6 CSRs)
- **Pain:** Team can't see per-page / per-rider RTS; needs role-based dashboards
- **Budget:** ₱30k–₱150k/month
- **Where they are:** Industry meetups, LinkedIn, referrals from agencies

### Tertiary Segment: The "Agency / Partner"
- **Who:** Dropship mentors, fulfillment agencies, COD consultants
- **Opportunity:** White-label / affiliate — they install Artemis across their roster
- **Where they are:** Private mastermind groups, Discord servers, YouTube creators

## 3. Positioning & Messaging

### Positioning Statement
> For Filipino COD sellers on Pancake who are bleeding profit to return-to-sender, Artemis is the analytics & automation platform that shows the real RTS number and moves it — unlike spreadsheets and generic dashboards, Artemis is built specifically for how PH COD actually runs.

## 5. Channels & Tactics

### A. Organic Social — Facebook & TikTok (priority)
The PH COD ecosystem lives on Facebook groups and TikTok. This is where the primary audience hangs out.

- **Facebook presence:**
  - Artemis Page with 3x/week posts: RTS stats, seller stories, how-to snippets
  - Join + publish (with permission) in *Pancake Seller PH*, *COD Seller Philippines*, *Online Sellers PH*
  - Private Artemis Community group → user-generated tips, RTS leaderboards, Q&A
- **TikTok:**
  - Short-form (<60s) "RTS autopsy" videos — break down a real anonymized seller's dashboard
  - "POV: nakita mo finally ang totoong RTS mo" (Taglish hook)
  - Hashtags: `#codsellerph` `#onlineseller` `#pancakepos` `#ecomph`
- **Instagram/Threads:** repurpose TikTok content, carousels of case-study stats

### B. Content Marketing — `/blog` + SEO
Own the PH COD keyword space.

- **Target queries:** "how to reduce RTS Philippines," "Pancake POS analytics," "COD return rate Philippines," "paano bawasan ang RTS"
- **Pillar content (cornerstone):**
  1. "The 2026 Philippine COD RTS Benchmark Report" — original data, gated for email
  2. "The Complete Guide to Reducing RTS for Pancake Sellers"
  3. "Parcel Journey Notifications: The One Setting That Cuts RTS by 20%+"
- **Cadence:** 2 posts/week (1 educational, 1 case study / benchmark)
- **Content format mix:** long-form guides, case studies (with the anonymized brands from the welcome page), calculators, comparison tables

### C. Paid Acquisition
Start small, scale what converts.

| Channel | Why | Monthly budget (M1→M12) |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook/Instagram Ads** | Highest intent for PH COD sellers | ₱30k → ₱200k |
| **TikTok Ads** | Video-first, cheap reach, matches audience | ₱15k → ₱100k |
| **Google Search** | Capture intent on "Pancake analytics," "reduce RTS" | ₱10k → ₱60k |
| **YouTube pre-roll** | Target PH e-comm creator audiences | ₱0 → ₱30k (month 6+) |

**Ad creative angles (tested in parallel):**
1. "Calculate your RTS bleed" → `/rts-calculator` (lead magnet)
2. Case-study video: "32.95% → 17.21% in 3 months"
3. "Your RTS is a number. Let's move it." (brand)
4. Before/after dashboard demo (15s screen-record)

### D. Partnerships & Affiliate
The multiplier channel.

- **Pancake ecosystem partnership:** pursue official listing/marketplace integration
- **Courier partnerships:** J&T, Flash, Ninja Van — co-branded webinars on RTS reduction (their interest is aligned: fewer failed deliveries)
- **Affiliate program for COD mentors/agencies:** 20% recurring commission, dashboard-accessible. Recruit 10 founding affiliates by month 3.
- **Creator partnerships:** sponsor PH e-comm YouTubers (Omar Shantti-type audience) for long-form reviews

### E. Community & Events
- **Monthly Artemis livestream:** "RTS Office Hours" — live seller dashboard audits
- **Quarterly in-person meetups:** Manila, Cebu, Davao — 20–40 sellers, RTS workshop
- **Sponsor 1–2 PH e-comm conferences/year** (month 6+)

### F. Email / Lifecycle
- **Lead magnet email sequence (7 days):** RTS Calculator result → educational → case study → trial CTA
- **Trial-in-progress sequence (14 days):** Day 0 onboarding checklist → Day 3 "connect Parcel Journey" → Day 7 ROI snapshot → Day 12 conversion offer
- **Paid-user retention:** monthly "RTS report card" email with their trend + tips

## 6. Funnel & Conversion Strategy

```
Ad / Social / SEO  ──►  /rts-calculator  ──►  Email sequence  ──►  Free trial  ──►  Paid
                       (lead magnet)                              (14 days)
                                                                      │
                                                                      ▼
                                                              Activate: Parcel Journey ON
                                                                      │
                                                                      ▼
                                                                Retained customer
```

### Critical Conversion Moments
1. **Landing → Calculator:** headline + "Calculate your RTS bleed" CTA (already on welcome page)
2. **Calculator → Signup:** show the ₱ bleed, then: "Start free trial — see your real number in 2 minutes"
3. **Signup → Pancake Connect:** this is the biggest drop-off. Need: video walkthrough, chat support, "we'll do it with you" option
4. **Connect → Parcel Journey ON:** our activation metric. Guided tour on Day 2 of trial.
5. **Trial Day 10 → Paid:** show month-over-month RTS trend in-app + personalized email from founder

### Activation Definition
A workspace is **activated** when:
- ✅ Pancake page connected
- ✅ ≥100 orders synced
- ✅ Parcel Journey notifications enabled
- ✅ At least 1 team member invited

Target: 60% of trials hit activation.

## 10. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Pancake changes API / restricts integration | Deepen direct partnership; diversify to Shopify/WooCommerce COD down the line |
| A competitor launches similar product | Double down on community + case-study moat; speed of shipping |
| Seller can't activate alone (drop-off at Pancake connect) | Live chat support during trial; "we'll set it up for you" white-glove onboarding |
| Case study results don't replicate broadly | Be transparent: "Results vary." Publish median outcomes, not best-case only |
| Ad fatigue on FB / TikTok | Rotate 8–12 creatives/month; invest in UGC from real sellers |
| Pricing pushback from small sellers | Free tier for <200 orders/month as acquisition; paid unlocks at scale |
