# Artemis · Post Calendar

> 90-day posting schedule across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and community groups. Every slot maps to a piece in `content.md` or a theme to create.

**All posts must follow the Artemis brand system — see `content.md` "Brand Block" before publishing.**

---

## Posting Principles

1. **Consistency beats volume.** 3 high-quality posts/week per primary platform > 10 mediocre ones.
2. **Platform-native first.** TikTok vertical, FB feed mixed, IG carousel-heavy, groups text-first.
3. **PH time zones matter.** Primary windows: **11:30am–1:00pm** (lunch scroll) and **7:00pm–9:30pm** (evening peak).
4. **Test, don't guess.** Every post tagged with hypothesis + outcome; review weekly.
5. **One CTA per post.** Calculator OR trial OR group — never three.

## Approval & Publishing Workflow

1. **Creator drafts** copy + image prompt (in `content.md` format)
2. **Image generated** via ChatGPT/DALL-E using full Brand Block
3. **Brand check** using the checklist in `content.md`
4. **Schedule** in Buffer / Meta Business Suite / native scheduler
5. **Post-publish** log metrics in weekly tracker after 48h
6. **Friday review** — kill underperformers, 2x the winners
